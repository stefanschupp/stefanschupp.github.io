abstract: "This report is concerned with a friendly competition for formal verification and policy synthesis of stochastic models. The main goal of the report is to introduce new benchmarks and their properties within this category and recommend next steps toward next year's edition of the competition. Given that the tools for stochastic models are at their early stages of development compared to those of non-probabilistic models, the main focus is to report on an initiative to collect a set of minimal benchmarks that all such tools can run, thus facilitating the comparison between the efficiency of the implemented techniques. This friendly competition took place as part of the workshop Applied Verification for Continuous and Hybrid Systems (ARCH) in Summer 2023."
